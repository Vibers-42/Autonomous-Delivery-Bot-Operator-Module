Problem Statement

Current delivery robots can usually reach the apartment building entrance, but they cannot complete the final step of the delivery because they cannot independently use elevators.

As a result:

Customers must come downstairs to collect orders.
Human intervention is still required for high-rise deliveries.
True door-to-door autonomous delivery is not possible.
Navix Solution

Develop a Smart Elevator Bridge System that allows Navix to communicate with a building's elevator controller and autonomously travel between floors without pressing physical buttons.

This enables:

Fully autonomous apartment deliveries.
Reduced dependence on human delivery personnel.
Seamless door-to-door package delivery in multi-story residential buildings.
Elevator Bridge Workflow
Step 1: Delivery Assigned

Customer places an order.

Warehouse
   ↓
Navix
   ↓
Apartment Building

Navix receives:

Building ID
Apartment Number
Destination Floor

Example:

Building A
Flat 805
Floor 8
Step 2: Navix Reaches Elevator Lobby

After entering the building and passing security:

Navix → Elevator Lobby

Navix identifies:

Current floor
Elevator location
Destination floor
Step 3: Elevator Request

Navix sends a request to the Smart Elevator Bridge.

Navix
   ↓ Wi-Fi
Elevator Bridge

Request:

Robot ID: NVX-101
Current Floor: G
Destination Floor: 8
Step 4: Authentication

The Elevator Bridge verifies:

Is this a valid Navix robot?
Is delivery authorized?
Is building access granted?

If approved:

Access Granted
Step 5: Elevator Called

Bridge sends command to elevator controller.

Elevator Bridge
      ↓
Elevator Controller
      ↓
Call Elevator to Ground Floor

The elevator arrives automatically.

Step 6: Navix Enters Elevator

Bridge informs Navix:

Elevator Ready
Doors Open

Navix uses:

LiDAR
Camera
Ultrasonic sensors

to safely enter.

Step 7: Destination Floor Selection

After entering:

Navix
   ↓
Bridge
   ↓
Elevator Controller

Command:

Go To Floor 8

The elevator begins moving.

Step 8: Floor Monitoring

While moving:

Elevator Controller
      ↓
Bridge
      ↓
Navix

Updates:

Current Floor = 3
Current Floor = 5
Current Floor = 7
Current Floor = 8

Navix knows exactly where it is.

Step 9: Arrival

Bridge receives:

Reached Floor 8

Bridge sends:

Destination Reached
Doors Open

Navix exits.

Step 10: Final Delivery

Navix navigates through the corridor.

Floor 8
   ↓
Flat 805

Customer receives OTP.

Package compartment unlocks.

Delivery completed.
